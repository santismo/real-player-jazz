Listen to an established reference

Reference recordings prioritize a notable, historically established performance that presents the tune’s head. Each selection includes an artist, release, reason and source links. A matching title or available automatic transcription is not enough.

The reference starts at the opening. Its playback is independent of the MIDI head study unless you supply timing yourself. Existing synced studies remain available in the Recording menu. Direct listening review and exact head timestamps are still pending for some references; see each recording’s details. Unreviewed tunes are labeled “Reference selection pending.”

Practice and accuracy

Curated historical references load first; explicit artist or part choices retain their study recording. For tunes without a reference, matched heads load first. If only a solo or automatic line has a matched recording, it loads first and the Head button can open the separate melody study. Select an artist or part to keep that choice. Slow down, drag the tab, use the seek slider, jump five seconds, or set A and B to loop. Random uses the current library or catalog filter and search, skips entries without notes, and avoids repeats when another result is available. Mobile keeps video, controls, tab and fretboard together; Library and Settings open separately.

The recording's clock drives the tab and fretboard. Source timing is checked structurally, but every upload has not received an independent listening audit. JazzTube offsets have approximately 0.2-second resolution. Upload changes, regional restrictions and YouTube buffering can affect playback. Advanced timing adjustments remain available.

Parker head/video pairs use long matching pitch sequences and fitted note-onset timing to connect the Omnibook transcription with a published JazzTube performance. Two anchors retain measured speed differences. This is inferred same-performance alignment, not direct YouTube audio verification. Head video audit.

Guitar fingerings are usually suggested arrangements. Valid contributor string/fret positions are retained for Joe Pass score studies, but actual artist fingering is not independently verified. Crowded tab columns widen, and scrubbing follows their spacing. Octave shifts and unplayable voicings are reported. No missing performance chords are invented; generic MIDI chart placement is labeled estimated.

Joe Pass contributor scores may contain unverified repeats or expressive notation. Jeannine's long repeat and four artificial harmonics in The Shadow of Your Smile still need review. Estimated audio matches are labeled separately from source alignments.

Imported MIDI stays in your browser session. Favorites and optional custom timing points are stored locally. Recording playback and thumbnails require internet access.
